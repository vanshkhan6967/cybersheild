🛡️ Universal Guidelines

The 5 Golden Rules of Digital Safety

Protecting yourself online doesn't require technical degrees. Follow these 5 fundamental principles in your everyday digital life to stay safe from scammers, hackers, and privacy leaks.

🔑
Rule #1

1 Account = 1 Unique Password

Never reuse the same password across multiple sites. If one site gets breached, hackers try that same password on your bank, email, and social accounts!

🤫
Rule #2

OTPs & PINs are Top Secret

Bank staff, police, and customer support will NEVER ask for your One-Time Password (OTP) or card PIN. Never share them over phone calls or texts!

🔍
Rule #3

Verify Sender Before You Click

Unexpected package tracking SMS? Bank emergency email? Always check the exact web address link and sender phone number before tapping any links.

📸
Rule #4

Pause & Check Backgrounds

Before posting photos or memes, zoom in on the background. Check for street signs, house numbers, work IDs, or school logos that reveal where you live or work.

🧹
Rule #5

Clean Up Your Trail

Delete unused apps, keep social media profiles set to 'Private', and clean up old inactive online accounts to minimize your digital footprint.

⚖️ Real-Life DOs & DON'Ts Cheat Sheet

✓ ALWAYS DO

  • • Turn on Two-Step Verification (2FA) on your WhatsApp, Email, and Banking apps.
  • • Call your bank directly using the phone number printed on your card if you get suspicious SMS alerts.
  • • Use a trusted Password Manager app to remember long, strong passwords.
  • • Keep your smartphone's operating system and apps updated to the latest version.

✕ NEVER DO

  • • Never answer phone callers asking you to install 'remote control' repair apps (like AnyDesk or TeamViewer).
  • • Never post pictures of your concert tickets, boarding passes, or new house keys.
  • • Never grant simple utility apps (like calculators or flashlights) access to your phone contacts.
  • • Never answer viral social media quizzes asking for your birth city or pet's name.
